### Q 1

He is a man of <u>few</u> words. Here the underlined word is-

* Demonstrative adjective
* Descriptive adjective
* Distributive adjective
* Indefinite numeral adjective

## answer
D

## explanation
The underlined word "few" modifies the noun "words" by indicating an indefinite number of things rather than a specific amount or a quality, which makes it an adjective of number (numeral adjective). Since it does not specify an exact count, it is classified specifically as an **indefinite numeral adjective**, ruling out demonstrative, descriptive, and distributive adjectives.
